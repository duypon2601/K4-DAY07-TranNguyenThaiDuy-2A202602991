# UTSC Library Borrowing Policy

You will need a TCard or library card to borrow from the libraries. If you lose your TCard, you must report it to the library to ensure material is not charged to your account. TCards are issued by the TCard office. You can add funds to your TCard using TCard+.

## Loan Privileges by Patron Type

Loan privileges by patron type at most University of Toronto Libraries:

| Patron Type | Loan Period | Maximum Potential Loan Period | Item Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Undergraduate students | 14 days | Unlimited | 50 items |
| Graduate students | 90 days | Unlimited | 200 items |
| Faculty members | 90 days | Unlimited | 200 items |
| Staff | 90 days | Unlimited | 200 items |
| Alumni | 14 days | 14 days (no renewals) | 25 items |
| Research readers | 14 days | 42 days | 100 items |
| Direct borrowers | 14 days | 42 days | 100 items |
| Associate members | 14 days | 42 days | 100 items |
| Continuing studies students | 14 days | Unlimited | 50 items |

### Exceptions and Special Collections
- **Short-term loans & course reserves:** Loan periods vary between 3, 5, 24, or 72 hours and cannot be renewed.
- **Popular collection:** Located in the Reading Room near the front entrance. Includes books, DVDs, magazines, video games, and board games. Loan period is 14 days with 1 renewal (maximum 14 days).
- **Periodicals:** 4-day loan period, no renewals, maximum 5 items.

## Renewals & Returns

### Unlimited Renewals
Current U of T students (including Continuing Education), faculty, and staff receive unlimited renewals for regular loans. Loans only become due if:
- Another user requests the checked out item (recall).
- There is a block on the user's account.
- The user's borrowing privileges expire.

*Not eligible for unlimited renewals:* Course reserves, short-term loans, technology items, audio/video items, Popular collection, and interlibrary loans.

### Automatic Renewals
Two days before an item is due, the system automatically attempts to renew it. Automatic renewals are not made for short-term loans, audio/video items, requested items, interlibrary loans, or alumni loans. If another borrower requests the item, the loan period shortens to 14 days.

### Returning Materials
Materials can be returned at the Information and Reference Desk. A drop box is located across from the desk when unstaffed, and an exterior drop box is available when the library is closed. Regular circulating items can be returned at any U of T library.

## Holds and Recalls

### Requesting an Item (Holds)
- Sign in to LibrarySearch, find the item, and select "REQUEST: Library Pick Up".
- Requested items are held for 7 days upon arrival.
- Concurrent hold request limits: Faculty, staff, and graduate students may place up to 25 concurrent requests. Other users (including undergraduate students) can place up to 10 concurrent requests.
- Short-term loan items and course reserves cannot have holds placed on them.

### Recalls
Any loaned item is subject to recall if requested by another borrower. Users must return recalled items within 14 days. Failure to return a recalled item within 14 days incurs a fine of $2.00 per day and blocks borrowing privileges.

## Fines and Fees

### Borrowing Blocks
Borrowing privileges are withheld when:
- An item is more than 14 days overdue.
- Total fines and fees reach $25.00 or greater.
- Short-term loan items or course reserves are more than 24 hours overdue.

### Overdue Fine Schedule
| Item Type | Fine Rate |
|---|---|
| Regular books and media | $0.50 per day per item |
| Course reserves and technology items | $0.50 per hour per item |
| Recalled items | $2.00 per day per item |

### Fees for Lost and Damaged Items
Items not returned within 42 days of the due date are deemed lost.

| Lost/Damaged Item Type | Fee / Cost |
|---|---|
| Lost book, CD, DVD, VHS, video or board game | $145.00 per item |
| Lost bound journal | $145.00 per item |
| Lost unbound journal | $75.00 per item |
| Lost charging cable, battery pack, headphones | $25.00 per item |
| Lost pico projector, camcorder, microphone | $300.00 per item |
| Lost laptop charger | $50.00 per item |
| Damaged item repair fee | $45.00 per item |
| Processing fee (if patron provides replacement copy with approval) | $45.00 per item |

### Payment Methods
Fines and fees can be paid online via LibrarySearch account, or in person at the Information and Reference Desk using Debit or Credit Card (VISA, MasterCard, AMEX). Cash is not accepted.
