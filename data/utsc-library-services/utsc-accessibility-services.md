# Services for Persons with Disabilities

The UTSC Library provides accessible spaces, assistive equipment, and personalized services to ensure equal access to library resources. Students with disabilities are encouraged to register with AccessAbility Services.

## Facilities and Physical Accessibility

### Entrances, Exits, and Automatic Doors
- An elevator is situated near the library entrance.
- Automatic door openers are installed at:
  - Library main entrance and washrooms
  - Group study rooms AC251A, AC252, AC255, AC284, AC285
  - Silent study room AC338
  - Mindfulness space AC258
  - Archives AC260
  - One Button Studio AC286

### Assistive Equipment and Adaptive Furniture
- **CCTV Magnifier:** Closed circuit television magnifier available for visually impaired users (reservation optional).
- **Ergonomic Seating & Desks:** Adjustable chair and height-adjustable table available upon request at the Info Desk.
- **Accessibility Software:** All Info Commons computers are installed with ZoomText (screen enlargement and text-to-speech) and Kurzweil 3000.

## Specialized Library Services

### Book Retrieval Assistance
Library staff provide retrieval assistance for patrons with disabilities. Requests can be made in person or by phone to the Information and Reference Desk (416-287-7500). Patrons must provide:
1. Book call number
2. Title
3. UTORid
Retrieved items are checked out and held at the desk for up to 3 days.

### Alternative Formats and Enlargements
- **Alternative Formats:** Course materials and publications can be produced in Braille, large print, electronic disc, or audiocassette through AccessAbility Services staff.
- **Course Reserves Enlargement:** Photocopies of course reserve readings can be enlarged upon request, with pickup available the following business day.

### Proxy Borrower Authorization
Borrowers unable to visit the library due to a disability may designate a proxy borrower to retrieve and check out materials on their behalf. A Pickup Authorization Form must be completed at the Information and Reference Desk. The primary account holder remains responsible for all borrowed materials.

### Safety and Evacuation Procedures
Patrons requiring physical assistance entering or exiting the library must notify the Information and Reference Desk of their arrival, location, and departure. Elevators do not operate during a fire emergency.
