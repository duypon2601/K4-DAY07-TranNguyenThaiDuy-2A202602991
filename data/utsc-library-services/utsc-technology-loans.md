# Technology Loans

The UTSC Library offers a variety of hardware, accessories, and audio-visual equipment for short-term borrowing to support coursework and academic projects.

## Overview and Borrowing Guidelines

### Item Categories and Pickup Locations
Technology items are organized into three pickup tiers:
1. **Self-Service Course Reserves Area:** Smaller accessories such as charging cables, adapters, and calculators.
2. **Information and Reference Desk (Info Desk):** Large electronic equipment including laptops, monitors, cameras, and projectors.
3. **Makerspace:** Specialized production equipment booked via MS Bookings. Some equipment is for in-space use only, while other items may be checked out.

### Return Procedures
- **Large electronic devices (laptops, monitors, cameras):** Must be returned directly to staff at the Info Desk.
- **Small items and cables:** Can be returned to the wooden "Book return" boxes located inside and outside the library entrance.
- **Makerspace equipment:** Must be returned directly to the Makerspace during operating hours.

### Fines and Loan Limits
- **Late return fine:** $0.50 per item per hour.
- **Renewal policy:** Technology items are short-term loans and cannot be renewed.

## Available Equipment Catalogue

### Computing and Display
- **Laptops and Monitors:** Available for checkout at the Info Desk.
- **Kodak Luma 150 Portable Wireless Projector:** Pico pocket projector with HDMI and Micro USB connections, 60 ANSI lumen brightness, 640x480 native resolution (supporting 4K), 2.5-hour rechargeable battery. Loan period: 3 hours. 1 available.
- **Webcam (Logitech QuickCam Pro 9000):** 2.0 MP, up to 1600x1200 video, USB 2.0. Loan period: 3 hours. 1 available.

### Audio and Visual Devices
- **Noise Canceling Headphones (Sony MDR-ZX110NC):** Standard headphone jack. Loan period: 3 hours.
- **Standard Over-Ear Headphones:** 3.5mm jack for computers and tablets. Loan period: 3 hours. 8 available.
- **Blu-ray Burner (BUFFALO Type-C USB 3.2):** Plays and burns Blu-Rays, DVDs, and CDs on Windows and Mac. Loan period: 72 hours. 1 available.
- **Blu-Ray Players:** Nutromo Blu-Ray players (5-hour loan, 2 available) and Sony SDP-S3100 Blu-ray player (72-hour loan, 1 available).
- **Makerspace Production Gear:** Wireless microphones for iOS/Android (3-day loan), Photo Studio Light Box Ring Light (3-day loan), Softbox Lighting Kit (3-day loan).

### Calculators
- **Scientific Calculator (TI-30Xa):** Battery-operated, 10-digit display, fraction and one-variable statistics features. Loan period: 3 hours. 4 available.
