# Ask a Librarian Chat Service

Ask a Librarian is a virtual reference chat service providing real-time research assistance from university librarians to students, faculty, and the public.

## Services Offered
Librarians can assist patrons remotely with:
- Finding academic sources, peer-reviewed articles, and research databases for specific topics.
- Locating specific books, journals, multimedia, or course materials across University of Toronto Libraries.
- Navigating library services, citation management tools, and online search platforms.

## Operating Hours (January 5 – April 11, 2026)
- **Monday – Thursday:** 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM
- **Friday:** 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Saturday – Sunday:** 12:00 PM – 6:00 PM

### Holiday Closures
The chat service is closed on:
- Monday, February 16, 2026 (Family Day)
- Friday, April 3, 2026 (Good Friday)

## Privacy and Transcripts
The service collects only the minimal information necessary to deliver assistance. At the conclusion of a chat session, patrons can print or email a complete transcript of the conversation for their research records.
