# Library Spaces and Facilities

The UTSC Library provides dedicated facilities for individual study, collaborative research, digital media creation, and experiential learning.

## Study Spaces and Rooms

### Silent Study Space (AC338)
A designated quiet study zone located in room AC338 for independent, distraction-free academic study.

### Group Study Rooms
The library features eight small bookable group study rooms equipped for collaborative group work. Rooms must be reserved in advance via the campus Intranet booking system.

### Mindfulness Space (AC258)
A calm room located in AC258 offering quiet seating and wellness amenities designed to support student mental health and relaxation.

## Creative and Media Production Studios

### Makerspace
An open hands-on facility supporting digital fabrication, rapid prototyping, and creative exploration. Equipment includes 3D printers, laser cutters, and crafting tools.

### One Button Studio (AC286)
A simplified, automated digital recording facility where students and faculty can record high-definition video presentations at the touch of a single button.

### Podcast Room (AC252A)
A sound-dampened recording suite in room AC252A equipped with an audio mixer, broadcast microphones, and studio headphones for podcast production, voice-overs, and audio streaming.

## Specialized Academic Facilities and Services

### Archives and Special Collections (AC260)
Preserves rare books, historical manuscripts, campus records, and unique cultural collections supporting scholarly research. Open by appointment.

### The BRIDGE
An advanced facility developed in partnership between the UTSC Library and the Department of Management, integrating business research, financial data terminals, and entrepreneurship support.

### Course Reserves and Short-Term Loan Area
Located 20 steps to the left of the main Information Desk, housing high-demand assigned course readings and tech accessories.

### Community Hubs
- **Reuse Depot:** A student-run exchange hub to share stationery, surplus notebooks, pens, and school supplies.
- **Seed Library:** Located opposite the main floor washrooms, offering free seeds for community gardening and agricultural sustainability.
