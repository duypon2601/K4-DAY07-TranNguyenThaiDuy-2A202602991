# Course Reserves and Short-Term Loans

Course reserves are instructional materials set aside for specific courses to ensure equitable access for students.

## What Are Course Reserves?
Instructors can make required course readings available through two formats:
1. **Online readings:** Accessible through Quercus, the University's online learning management system.
2. **Physical items:** Available for borrowing from the Course Reserves / Short-Term Loan section at the UTSC Library.

## Physical Course Reserves
- **Location:** Physical course reserves are located 20 steps to the left of the Information and Reference Desk (InfoDesk) on the main floor of the UTSC Library.
- **Loan terms:** Loan periods for short-term loan items vary between 3 hours, 5 hours, 24 hours, or 72 hours. These items cannot be renewed, and holds cannot be placed on them.
- **Overdue fines:** Overdue fines for course reserves and short-term loans are $0.50 per hour per item.

## Online Course Reserves (Library Reading List in Quercus)
Instructors make digital course materials (articles, book chapters, web links, and media resources) accessible via the Library Reading List module on Quercus:
1. Navigate to your course page in Quercus.
2. Select the "Library Reading List" app in the course navigation menu.
3. Select the enrolled course to open the full list of readings.
4. For assistance accessing digital readings, consult the Library Reading List guide or contact your liaison librarian.

## Contact Information
- For questions or feedback regarding course reading lists, email: `readinglist-library@utsc.utoronto.ca`.
- Instructors can submit course reserve requests through the instructor portal.
