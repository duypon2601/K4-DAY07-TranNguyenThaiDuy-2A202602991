# Research and Publishing Services for Faculty

The UTSC Library partners with faculty and researchers across every phase of the research cycle, from proposal drafting and literature synthesis to open access publishing and research data archiving.

## Research Consultations and Specialist Support

### In-Depth Research Consultations
Faculty can book consultations with specialist librarians:
- **Liaison Librarians:** Department-specific subject specialists advising on literature search strategies, collection purchasing, and course resource integration.
- **Digital Scholarship Librarians:** Provide technical guidance on digital methods, data manipulation, text mining, and computational research workflows.
- **Entrepreneurship & Innovation Librarians:** Assist with market research, intellectual property, and commercial venture data.

### Classroom Research Integration
Liaison Librarians regularly teach discipline-specific research and information literacy sessions directly in faculty classrooms and collaborate on multi-year student-faculty research initiatives.

## Publishing, Repositories, and Open Access

### TSpace Institutional Repository
TSpace is a free, secure open access research repository managed by University of Toronto Libraries to disseminate, preserve, and showcase faculty and graduate research outputs.

### Journal Production Services
Provides free open access hosting, indexing assistance, and technical infrastructure for peer-reviewed academic journals edited by faculty members.

### Pressbooks Open Publishing
An open publishing platform integrated into eCampusOntario, allowing faculty to author, customize, and publish accessible digital open educational resources (OER) and open textbooks.

### Borealis (U of T Dataverse)
A secure Canadian multi-disciplinary research data repository supporting Research Data Management (RDM), FAIR data principles, data deposit compliance, and research dataset preservation.

## Document Delivery and Access Services

### InfoExpress Delivery Service
InfoExpress is a premium document delivery service provided exclusively to UTSC faculty, staff, and graduate students. Staff retrieve physical books, journal articles, and microforms from UTSC, other U of T campus libraries, or partner institutions worldwide and deliver them directly to the faculty member's departmental office or digital inbox.

### Highland Hall Storage Stacks Browsing
Faculty members and authorized researchers can schedule browsing appointments to access off-site collections stored in the Highland Hall storage stacks.

## Research Grant Support
Library services, staff expertise, and repository hosting can be designated as formal in-kind contributions within major tri-agency grant proposals (SSHRC, NSERC, CIHR). Liaison Librarians assist in estimating infrastructure and data curation costs.
